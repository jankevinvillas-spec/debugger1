ARABICA COFFEE

Smooth and Aromatic

Arabica is known for its *smooth, aromatic, and slightly sweet flavor*. It may have fruity, floral, nutty, or chocolatey notes and is generally less bitter than other coffee types. It grows best in *cool, high-altitude areas*.

In the Philippines, Arabica is grown in places such as *Benguet, Sagada, Kalinga, and Davao*. It is commonly used for *specialty coffee, premium blends, and espresso*.
