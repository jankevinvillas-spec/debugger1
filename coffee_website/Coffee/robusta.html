ROBUSTA COFFEE

Strong and Bold

Robusta is known for its *strong, bold, and earthy flavor*. It contains more caffeine than Arabica and usually has a stronger and more bitter taste. It grows well in *warm, low-altitude areas* and is generally more resistant to pests and diseases.

Robusta is commonly used in *instant coffee, commercial coffee products, espresso blends, and strong brewed coffee*.
