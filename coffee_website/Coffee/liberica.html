Liberica COFFEE

Unique and Aromatic

Liberica is known for its *unique, aromatic, and complex flavor*. It has a distinct, floral scent and a rich, full-bodied taste. It grows best in *tropical regions* and is less common than other coffee types.

Liberica is commonly used in *specialty coffee, premium blends, and traditional brewing methods*.
