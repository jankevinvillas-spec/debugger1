ABOUT COFFEE

From Bean to Cup

Coffee begins as a plant that produces coffee cherries. The beans are removed, dried, roasted, ground, and finally brewed.

The Philippines has several coffee-growing areas and is known for coffee varieties such as Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, and Excelsa.
