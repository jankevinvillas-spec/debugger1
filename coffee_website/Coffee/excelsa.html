Excelsa COFFEE

Distinct and Bold

Excelsa is known for its *distinct, bold, and fruity flavor*. It has a unique, complex taste with notes of dark fruit and a rich, full-bodied profile. It grows best in *tropical regions* and is less common than other coffee types.

Excelsa is commonly used in *specialty coffee, premium blends, and traditional brewing methods*.
